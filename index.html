INDEXED UNIVERSAL LIFE

Protection today.
Possibilities tomorrow.

The smart way to build wealth & protect your legacy. Unlock the power of Indexed Universal Life (IUL).

Protect your family. Build your future. All in one place. Why settle for “just” life insurance? Explore protection for the “what ifs” and a financial strategy for the “what’s next.”

Index-linked growth

Discuss participating in index gains with protection against negative index crediting. Policy charges still apply; cash value can decline.

Tax-advantaged growth

Explore cash value accumulation for retirement, emergencies or business needs. Tax treatment depends on policy structure and circumstances.

A lasting legacy

Life insurance can provide a death benefit to loved ones, subject to policy terms and exclusions.

Access to cash value

Ask about withdrawals and policy loans, including their costs and effects on benefits and taxes.

Discuss safety, growth and your long-term financial strategy with MMT.

FIXED INDEXED ANNUITIES

A floor beneath
your future.

Secure your retirement. Explore guaranteed income with a fixed indexed annuity.

Tired of watching the market ride a roller coaster with your life savings? An FIA is designed for people seeking a balance of protection and growth.

Principal protection

Protection from market downturns under contract terms. Withdrawals, surrender charges and other adjustments can affect the amount received.

A personal pension

Explore lifetime income options for you and your spouse. Availability, costs and guarantees depend on the contract and riders.

Tax-deferred growth

Interest can accumulate tax-deferred until distributions are taken.

Market-linked interest

Interest may be linked to an index such as the S&P 500, without investing directly in that index. Caps and other limits may apply.
